# Changelog

## 1.7.33 · 24 November 2026

### Designer
- Disabled components appear faded so you can select them and turn them on. The component picker follows the plate you are editing, and NPC plates keep threat controls under Threat.
- Right-click a selected component to reset its position. With several components selected, the same click resets their positions together. The guide now distinguishes a click from a drag.
- Aura previews show multiple buffs, debuffs and crowd-control effects. Selecting a row highlights the whole row. Rows snap side by side, and placement accounts for nearby bars, distance and threat labels, and aura timers.
- Improved timer placement while dragging and corrected the initial spacing of debuffs after a reload.

### Auras
- Buffs, debuffs and crowd control each have their own border colour and thickness in Auras and the Designer. Choose 0 px to hide a border. Crowd control defaults to purple so you can distinguish it from red debuffs.

### Settings and previews
- Related options sit under headings such as Guild, Resources and Party markers. Search includes section names, and scrollbar thumbs reflect how much content is visible.
- Scroll to zoom toward the cursor and left-drag to pan in compact or expanded settings previews.
- Target highlighting and target scale appear in the Targeting settings preview. Reaction and threat colour samples stay on Colours. The uninterruptible cast sample is available on Castbar and Colours.
- The party member icon defaults to on for new profiles and after a reset. Reset all also refreshes the checkboxes and other controls before reloading.
