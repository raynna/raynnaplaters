# Changelog

## 1.7.4 · 24 November 2026

- Plate-wide controls appear at the top of the designer. Personal includes an Enabled checkbox.
- The designer now shows settings for the selected component. Select Name for name size and wrapping, or Threat for its visibility, position and four threat colours.
- Box-select components and drag them together. Right-drag a component to resize it or right-drag empty space to pan. Arrow keys nudge the selection's active component. Clicking empty space clears the selection.
- Selected components appear in a compact sidebar. Click a header to expand its controls inline. Use colour swatches to adjust bar fills, backgrounds and uninterruptible casts.
- Mark party members with an icon, custom text or colours. Raid markers are optional. Configure these under Party or Friendly players.
- Settings categories now run down the left side. Cast colours are available under both Castbar and Colours.
