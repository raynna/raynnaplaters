# Raynna's Nameplates 1.4.46

- Completed kill and collection objectives now clear the quest colour when objective progress is available. NPCs stay highlighted if they contribute to another unfinished objective.
- Friendly pets and minions have compact, text-only plates outside combat, with an option to hide the companion text.
- Idle non-interactive friendly NPCs show only their name by default. Quest and service NPCs retain their compact indicators outside combat.
